Sick of filling out forms? Fillkin does it for you.

Fill in your Fillkin card once, plus a card for each of your children. Then, whenever a form asks for the same details again – school trips, a new doctor, car insurance, the vet – they're ready in seconds.

USE YOUR DETAILS ANYWHERE
• Tap Copy next to any detail and paste it straight into a form, in any app or website.
• Share as PDF: a neat one-page summary to save, print or email.
• Share as text: send the details by message.
• You choose what's included every time. Passport and licence details stay off unless you switch them on.

ONE CARD FOR EVERY PERSON
• Your card: name, date of birth, address, contact details, work, car, passport and driving licence, GP surgery, NHS number and hospital number, emergency contact, pets and bank accounts.
• Children's cards: school, doctor, passport and emergency contact – with your address and GP filled in for you.
• Add a photo and colour to each card.
• Gentle reminders to check children's cards, so school and doctor details stay up to date.

LOCKED ON YOUR PHONE
• Everything is encrypted on your iPhone with a 10-digit passcode. Unlock with Face ID if you like.
• No account, no sign-in, no adverts and no tracking. We never see your details.
• Your cards are kept out of iCloud backups. Make your own locked backup file whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone with your backup password.
• Fillkin hides your details when you switch away, and locks itself after a minute.

PRACTISE FIRST
Try "Enter with Fillkin" on pretend shop, school, doctor, car insurance and vet forms to see how it works, before you use it for real.

HISTORY
See what you copied or shared, and when – kept on your phone only.
