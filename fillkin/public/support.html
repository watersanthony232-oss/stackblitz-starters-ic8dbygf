Fillkin Help

Sick of filling out forms? Fillkin does it for you.

Need help, or found a problem?
Email tony@tozindustries.com. The quickest way to show a problem is to record your screen while it happens and send the video. I'll do my utmost to sort it within a day.

Getting started

Choose a 10-digit passcode, then fill in your card. Add a card for each child with + Add a child. You can turn on Face ID in Settings.

Using your details on a form

Go to Share, switch on what you want to share, then tap Use on a real form. Tap Copy next to a detail and paste it into the form, or send everything as a PDF or text with Share as PDF or Share as text.

I forgot my passcode

Nobody can open your cards without it, not even us. Tap Forgot passcode? on the lock screen to erase Fillkin and start again. If you made a locked backup, you can restore it afterwards with your backup password.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your cards are kept on your iPhone only and are not in iCloud backups. Before you change phone, go to Settings › Make a backup and save the file somewhere safe (for example iCloud Drive or email). On the new phone, install Fillkin and use Restore from a backup.
