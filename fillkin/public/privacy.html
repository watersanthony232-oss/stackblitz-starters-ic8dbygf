Fillkin Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

In short: everything you put into Fillkin stays on your iPhone, locked. Fillkin has no account and no servers, so we never see, collect, sell or share your details. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

Who we are

Fillkin is made by Anthony Waters, trading as Aurora Glance (part of Toz Industries), Southampton, United Kingdom. Contact: tony@tozindustries.com.

What Fillkin keeps

Only the details you choose to type in, for you and your children, so you can use them on forms. Depending on what you add, this can include:

  • names, dates of birth, home address, email, mobile number and an emergency contact
  • job and employer, car registration, make and model, and your pets' names
  • school, GP surgery, NHS number and hospital number
  • passport and driving licence numbers and expiry dates
  • bank account nickname, name on the account, sort code and account number
  • a photo and colour for each card, if you add them
  • a history of when you copied or shared details, kept on your phone so you can see it

Fillkin does not hold payment cards and cannot move money.

Where it is kept

  • On your iPhone only. Your details are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a random key. That key is locked with your 10-digit passcode, which Fillkin never stores.
  • The locked file is also protected by iOS whenever your phone is locked, and it is kept out of iCloud and computer backups, so it doesn't leave your iPhone that way.
  • If you turn on Face ID (or Touch ID), the key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, for this device only, behind Face ID. Fillkin never sees or stores your face or fingerprint; iOS checks it.
  • Fillkin has no sign-in, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking, and uses no third-party services that receive your details.

When you use your details

  • Copy: when you tap Copy, that one detail goes on your iPhone's clipboard so you can paste it into a form. Other apps you paste into can read what's on the clipboard, as with anything you copy.
  • Share as PDF or text: Fillkin makes the summary on your phone and opens the iPhone share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Files, email or a message). It is only sent if you send it, and only to where you choose. A shared summary is not locked, so only share it with people you trust. We don't receive a copy.
  • You choose what to include every time, on the Share screen. Passport and licence details are off unless you switch them on.

Backups you make

If you make a backup in Settings, Fillkin saves one file, locked with a backup password you choose (PBKDF2 and AES-256-GCM). You decide where the file goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. Without the password nobody can open it, not even us, and we can't recover a lost password.

Children

Fillkin is for adults to use, including to keep their children's details. It is not aimed at children, and a child's details stay on the parent's phone.

Keeping and deleting your details

  • Change or delete any detail in the app at any time.
  • Settings › Erase Fillkin from this iPhone deletes all your cards and the Face ID key straight away.
  • Deleting the Fillkin app also deletes everything it holds on the phone. Backup or summary files you saved somewhere else stay where you put them, and you can delete them there.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have rights over your information. Because we hold none of it, there is nothing we can show, change or delete for you, but you can always ask us at tony@tozindustries.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
