Simple Zone has no account and no sign-in. It shows local businesses from a published list (a public Google Sheet that we manage), so no login or demo account is needed.

To try it:
1. Open the app. The list of businesses appears on the home screen.
2. Type something in "What do you need?" and tap Search, or tap a quick search such as "coffee".
3. Tap a business to see its page. Call, WhatsApp, Directions and Visit website open the Phone, WhatsApp, Maps and Safari apps.
4. Tap "Near me – nearest first" to sort by distance. Location is only used on the device for this, and is never stored or sent anywhere.
5. "My shopping list" finds everything on the list in the fewest stops.
6. "About" (top right) has the privacy policy link. "Add your business" explains free listings and opens our website.

The app collects no data, has no adverts, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. With no internet, it shows the last list it saved.
