Local first – less driving, more living.

Simple Zone helps you find what you need from local businesses near you. Type what you need, such as coffee, a haircut or a phone charger, and Simple Zone shows the businesses that have it, nearest first.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Search for what you need, or tap a quick search
• Near me: see the nearest businesses first (your location stays on your phone)
• See whether a business is open now, and its opening times
• Filter by area, category, open now, open late and open on Sunday
• Call, WhatsApp, get directions or visit the website in one tap
• My shopping list: put in everything you need and Simple Zone finds it all in the fewest stops

EASY TO READ
• Bigger text at one tap, and it follows your iPhone's text size
• Works with Dark Mode and VoiceOver

YOUR PRIVACY
• We never sell your data
• No accounts, no adverts, no tracking
• Your location is only used on your phone when you tap Near me, and is never stored or sent to us
• Your shopping list and settings stay on your phone

Simple Zone is starting in Southampton and across Hampshire. Local businesses can join for free.

Made by Aurora Glance, a small Southampton business.
