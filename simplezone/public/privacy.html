Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Simple Zone is made by Aurora Glance (part of Toz Industries), Southampton, UK.

What the app collects

Nothing about you. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

  • Location: only used when you tap Near me, on your phone, to sort shops by distance. It is never stored or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Shopping list and text size: saved only on your phone.

Links to other apps

Buttons like Call, WhatsApp, Directions and Visit website open other apps (Phone, WhatsApp, Maps, Safari). Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Businesses on Simple Zone

Business details shown in the app are the ones the business gave us to publish. Businesses that work from home can choose to show only their area. To change or remove a listing, email hello@auroraglance.com.
