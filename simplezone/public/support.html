Simple Zone help

Finding things

Type what you need, like “phone charger” or “haircut”, and tap Search. Add a place to search there, like “deli Stockport”. Tap Near me to put the nearest first.

Near me doesn't work

Open the iPhone Settings › Simple Zone › Location and choose While Using the App. Or pick your area from the list instead.

My shopping list

Put one thing on each line. Simple Zone finds the fewest businesses that have everything.
